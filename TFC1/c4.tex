\chapter{Research Proposal}

\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Dataset}

The frames belong to the drone recordings of a pineapple plantation of Upala Agr\'icola, in Costa Rica, captured from a top-down view at a two-meter altitude; individual frames of the continuous video streams were extracted and treated as still images \cite{Gonzalez2026,XieLi2025VAE}. % TODO: confirm with the advisor that this is exactly the subset used here (the file attributes only say that the source is a vertical-view collection of $1\,920\times1\,088$ frames).

The dataset used in this work contains 4\,908 frames, stored with their estimated depth maps and the bounding boxes of the pineapples (17\,609 boxes in total). The frames have a source resolution of $1\,920\times1\,088$ pixels and were reduced by an exact $2\times$ box average to $960\times544$. Depth is a monocular relative estimate, normalized per image, so it is neither metric nor comparable across images. The split follows the order of capture: blocks of 60 consecutive frames of a capture session are assigned whole to training, validation, or test, and 20 frames are discarded at every boundary between splits so that near-identical frames of a video do not leak across them; the frames marked as discarded belong to no split. This gives 3\,536 training, 381 validation, and 346 test frames.

\subsection{Pipeline}

The pipeline has two stages. A $\beta$-VAE compresses a $256\times256$ random crop of the frame, with the depth channel when it is part of the input, into a latent of shape $4\times32\times32$ \cite{Kingma2014,Higgins2017}. A generator then learns to produce latents from noise, and the decoder of the VAE turns them into images \cite{Rombach2022}. Two generators are used: DDPM, trained with 1\,000 steps \cite{Ho2020}, and Flow Matching with a linear path, sampled with 50 Euler steps \cite{Lipman2023}. Both use a convolutional U-Net without attention layers, which was shown to train stably on small homogeneous datasets \cite{Gonzalez2026}. The training recipe of the generators is the one that resulted from the audit of Chapter~V: 500 epochs with AdamW, a learning rate of $10^{-4}$ with a single cosine decay, gradient clipping at 1.0, and the scale of the latent estimated from 16 batches. Images are decoded with the mean of the output layer of the decoder, not with a sample. Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline} summarizes the pipeline: the factors act on the VAE, and the latents of the training set are used to train both generators.

\subsection{Evaluation}

The main response is the Fr\'echet Inception Distance (FID) of the generated images, and the secondary response is the Kernel Inception Distance (KID) \cite{Heusel2017,Binkowski2018}. The reconstruction of every VAE is also measured on the 346 test images with PSNR and SSIM, and with the FID of the reconstructed images, which is what is needed to answer whether a better reconstruction leads to a better generation (RQ3). The peak signal-to-noise ratio is computed from the mean squared error between an image $x$ and its reconstruction $\hat{x}$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{PSNR}=10\log_{10}\frac{\mathrm{MAX}^{2}}{\mathrm{MSE}(x,\hat{x})},
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{MAX}$ is the largest value of a pixel, here 1 because the images lie in $[0,1]$. The structural similarity index compares luminance, contrast, and structure in local windows \cite{Wang2004},
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{SSIM}(x,\hat{x})=\frac{(2\mu_x\mu_{\hat{x}}+c_1)(2\sigma_{x\hat{x}}+c_2)}{(\mu_x^{2}+\mu_{\hat{x}}^{2}+c_1)(\sigma_x^{2}+\sigma_{\hat{x}}^{2}+c_2)},
\end{equation}
with the local means $\mu$, variances $\sigma^{2}$, and covariance $\sigma_{x\hat{x}}$, and small constants $c_1$ and $c_2$ that stabilize the division. For both, higher is better, and they are reported separately for the color channels and for the depth channel. The reference set and the number of generated images are the same in every comparison. The FID computed on a finite sample is biased, and the bias depends on the model that is evaluated \cite{Chong2020}, so two models can be ranked differently only because of the size of their bias; for this reason the KID, which is unbiased, is analyzed in parallel, and a conclusion is drawn only when both agree.

\section{Design of Experiments}

\subsection{Strategy in Stages}

Six factors cannot be studied with every combination: a full factorial design would require $2^6=64$ cells, and each trained generator takes about 26 GPU hours. The experiment is therefore organized in stages, from a cheap screening to a confirmation of what matters.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Stage 0: measuring the noise.} Five replicates of the base cell, each with its own seed for the VAE and the generator. They give the standard deviation $\sigma$ of the FID between independent runs, which sets the power of the following stages and the margin used in the equivalence tests. The runs are reused as the base cell of Stage 1.
  \item \textbf{Stage 1: screening with DDPM.} Nothing is reused from the preliminary experiments: every VAE and every generator is trained from scratch with the corrected pipeline, and the preliminary results only inform the design. A fractional factorial design $2^{6-2}$ of resolution IV, with 16 cells and three replicates per cell (48 runs). It answers which of the six factors have an effect on the FID.
  \item \textbf{Stage 2: refinement.} A full factorial design with the factors that showed an effect, now with more levels where it makes sense (for example, 0, 25, and 50\% of masking).
  \item \textbf{Stage 3: confirmation with Flow Matching.} The factors that mattered are repeated with Flow Matching in a small full factorial design (for example, $2^3$ cells with two replicates), to check that the effect does not depend on the generator.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Fractional Factorial Design}

The $2^{6-2}$ design is generated by $E=ABC$ and $F=BCD$, with defining relation $I=ABCE=BCDF=ADEF$. Every word has four letters, so the design has resolution IV: the main effects, which are those of the hypothesis, are aliased only with interactions of three factors, and the interactions of two factors are aliased with one another in the groups $\{AB,CE\}$, $\{AC,BE\}$, $\{AD,EF\}$, $\{AF,DE\}$, $\{BD,CF\}$, $\{BF,CD\}$, and $\{AE,BC,DF\}$. The base cell, with every factor at its first level, belongs to the fraction. An effect of the adversarial loss that depends on the modality (the interaction $DF$) cannot be told apart from $AE$ and $BC$ in this stage; if it appears, Stage 2 resolves it.

Each replicate is a complete run of the pipeline with different seeds, from the training of the VAE to the training of the generator, so the variation of the VAE enters the estimate of the noise. The 400 images generated by one model give one FID, so they are not replicates: treating them as such is pseudoreplication \cite{Hurlbert1984}, and the unit of replication is the independently trained model. The order of the runs is randomized, and the node and GPU of each run are recorded.

\subsection{Statistical Analysis}

The analysis is fixed before the runs. For each stage, the FID is fitted with a linear model with the main effects and the estimable groups of interactions, and an analysis of variance of type II is computed. The assumptions are checked on the residuals with graphical diagnostics, the Shapiro--Wilk test for normality, and the Levene test for homogeneity of variance; if they fail, the FID is transformed (for example, with the square root or the logarithm) and the check is repeated. The significance level is $0.05$. In the screening of Stage 1, whose aim is not to miss factors that matter, the $p$-values of the main effects are adjusted by controlling the false discovery rate (Benjamini--Hochberg); in the confirmation of Stage 3, they are adjusted with the stricter Holm method. Comparisons between cells are made with Tukey's method, and every effect is reported with its confidence interval and not only with its $p$-value.

A non-significant result is not read as the absence of an effect. For each factor, an equivalence test (two one-sided tests) checks whether the effect lies within a margin $\Delta$ that is fixed after Stage 0 and before Stage 1; provisionally, $\Delta=5$ FID points, about 3\% of the FID of the base VAE. In this way, a factor without effect is reported as an effect smaller than $\Delta$, and not as an inconclusive result.

To answer RQ3, the reconstruction metrics and the FID of every cell are compared with a rank correlation across the cells, together with its confidence interval. The number of replicates follows a statistical power analysis, which relates the number of random seeds to the probabilities of the errors of the test \cite{Colas2018}, and the variance due to initialization and data order must be part of the noise estimate \cite{Bouthillier2021}; with few runs, intervals are preferred to point estimates \cite{Agarwal2021}. Three replicates per cell leave 32 degrees of freedom for the error, and, if the standard deviation between runs were 5 FID points, they would allow to detect main effects of about 4 points with a power of 0.8; the real value is obtained in Stage 0, and the number of replicates is adjusted if it differs.

\subsection{Budget}

Stage 1 requires 48 generators (about 26 hours each) and 48 VAEs trained from scratch (about 14 hours each), about 1\,900 GPU hours in total, which is about 20 days when four jobs run in parallel. Stage 0 adds two runs to the base cell, and Stage 3 about 16 generators. Because each checkpoint takes about 2.3~GB, every run is evaluated and then archived or deleted, so the storage quota of the cluster is not exceeded.

\subsection{Threats to Validity}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The study uses a single dataset of a single crop, and the depth is an estimate, not a measurement.
  \item The FID computed on 400 images is biased and noisy \cite{Chong2020}, which is why it is accompanied by the KID, by replicates, and by confidence intervals.
  \item The fractional design mixes some interactions of two factors; the groups are known in advance and resolved in Stage 2.
  \item The fixed parameters (for example, the weight of the adversarial loss and the number of epochs) may interact with the factors; they are reported so that the results can be interpreted within them.
  \item The conclusions about generation are conditioned on the DDPM of Stage 1 and are checked on Flow Matching only for the factors that mattered.
\end{itemize}
