\chapter*{\centering \large DECLARATION ON THE USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOOLS}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{DECLARATION ON THE USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE TOOLS}
\markboth{DECLARATION ON THE USE OF AI TOOLS}{DECLARATION ON THE USE OF AI TOOLS}

An artificial intelligence assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) was used as a support tool during the preparation of this proposal, for the following tasks: (i) drafting and improving the wording, grammar, and structure of the text; (ii) searching the literature and checking the bibliographic data of the references; (iii) reviewing the code of the experiments and assisting in running and examining them; (iv) comparing alternatives for the design of the experiments and their statistical analysis, among which the author chose; and (v) generating the diagrams of the document through scripts.

The research question, the hypothesis, the objectives, the scope, and the decisions on the design of the study were made by the author, who also reviewed and corrected the content and is responsible for all of it, including the references cited. The tool was not used to replace the work of the author in the development of the project.
